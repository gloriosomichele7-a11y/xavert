About the AI Task Brief Builder

This tool acts as a preparation layer between a person and an AI system. It does not generate the answer: it helps you formulate a precise, inspectable request.

Why it helps

  • Separates verified facts from context and preferences.
  • Defines the audience, format, tone and limits before generation.
  • Adds verification instructions to reduce confident but unsupported claims.
  • Keeps the drafting process local in your browser.
