About

The XAVERT QR Code Scanner & Reader decodes QR codes from image files, clipboard images and live camera frames.

Main Features

  • Scan QR codes from PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF images.
  • Use click, drag-and-drop or clipboard paste.
  • Scan continuously with a device camera.
  • Use native BarcodeDetector when available and jsQR as fallback.
  • Detect URLs, Wi-Fi, email, phone, SMS, geo, vCard and plain text.
  • Copy, open or download decoded content.

Privacy First

Images and camera frames are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to XAVERT. jsQR is downloaded from a public CDN only when the fallback decoder is needed.

Camera access requires HTTPS and browser permission. Detected URLs are never opened automatically.
