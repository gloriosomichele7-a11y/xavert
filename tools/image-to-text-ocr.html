About

The XAVERT Image to Text OCR extracts editable text from photos, screenshots, scans and other supported images directly in your browser.

Main Features

  • Extract text from JPG, PNG, WebP and BMP images.
  • Select up to three recognition languages for multilingual text.
  • Improve difficult images with grayscale, high-contrast, black-and-white, scaling, inversion and rotation controls.
  • Choose automatic, column, block, sparse-text, line or word recognition layouts.
  • Review overall confidence and individual low-confidence words.
  • Visualize detected word regions directly over the processed image.
  • Paste an image directly from the system clipboard when supported.
  • Copy extracted text or download TXT and structured OCR JSON.
  • Cancel active OCR processing when needed.

Privacy First

Your selected image is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to XAVERT. Tesseract.js, its WebAssembly OCR engine and the language data required for your selected languages are downloaded from third-party CDN infrastructure. Language data can be cached by your browser for later use.
