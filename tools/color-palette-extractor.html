About

The XAVERT Color Palette Extractor analyzes an image and identifies its dominant colors for graphic design, branding, user interfaces, websites and digital artwork.

Main Features

  • Extract 5, 8 or 10 visually distinct dominant colors.
  • View every color in HEX, RGB and HSL formats.
  • See the estimated visual coverage of each extracted color.
  • Copy individual colors or the complete palette.
  • Download palettes as TXT, JSON, CSS or SVG files.
  • Process JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF images.

Privacy First

Everything is processed locally within your browser. Your images and extracted colors are never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on any server.
