About

The XAVERT Regex Tester lets you test JavaScript-compatible regular expressions directly in your browser.

Main Features

  • Test regular expressions against custom text.
  • Use global, ignore-case and multiline flags.
  • Highlight matching text safely in the preview.
  • Use quick presets for numbers, words, URLs and email addresses.
  • Copy or download matched values.

Privacy First

Regex processing happens locally within your browser. Your pattern and test text are never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on any server.
