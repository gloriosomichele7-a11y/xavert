About the AI Response Reviewer

AI can produce useful work quickly, but fluent language is not evidence. This local tool helps identify claims, figures and sensitive content that should receive human attention.

What it checks

  • Strong certainty and absolute wording.
  • Numbers, prices, percentages and dates.
  • Presence or absence of URLs and source-like references.
  • Possible email addresses and phone numbers.
  • Whether the response appears to include assumptions or uncertainty.

It does not prove accuracy and must not replace professional review for medical, legal, financial or safety-critical decisions.
