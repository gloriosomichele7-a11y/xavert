About

The XAVERT Word & Character Counter analyzes text locally in your browser.

Main Features

  • Count words and total characters.
  • Count characters excluding whitespace.
  • Count sentences, paragraphs and lines.
  • Count spaces and unique words.
  • Calculate average word length.
  • Estimate reading and speaking time.
  • Calculate UTF-8 byte size.
  • Copy the original text instantly.

Privacy First

Text analysis happens locally within your browser. Your text is never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on any server.
