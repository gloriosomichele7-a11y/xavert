About

The XAVERT PDF Toolkit provides browser-based utilities for common PDF editing and conversion tasks.

Main Features

  • Merge, split, extract, delete, rotate and reorder PDF pages.
  • Convert JPG/PNG images to PDF and PDF pages to JPG/PNG.
  • Add watermarks and page numbers.
  • Compress image-heavy PDFs by rasterizing pages.
  • Add drawn or uploaded signatures.

Privacy First

PDF processing happens locally within your browser. Your files are never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on XAVERT servers.
