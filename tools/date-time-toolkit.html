About

The XAVERT Date & Time Toolkit combines practical date and time utilities in one browser-based workspace.

Main Features

  • Calculate the difference between two dates.
  • Calculate age in years, months, days and total days.
  • Convert Unix timestamps in seconds or milliseconds.
  • Generate the current Unix timestamp.
  • Convert dates and times between world time zones with DST support.
  • Add or subtract days from a selected date.
  • Calculate the remaining time until a future date and time.

Privacy First

Everything is processed locally within your browser. Your dates, timestamps and results are never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on any server.
