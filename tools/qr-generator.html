About

The XAVERT QR Code Generator creates QR codes locally in your browser for common data formats.

Main Features

  • Generate QR codes for text, URLs, email, phone and SMS.
  • Create Wi-Fi, vCard contact and calendar event QR codes.
  • Customize foreground and background colors.
  • Use transparent backgrounds when needed.
  • Download PNG, SVG, PDF and TXT files.
  • Copy QR data or the generated image.

Privacy First

QR code generation happens locally within your browser. Your QR data is never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on any server.
