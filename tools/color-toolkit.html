About

Color Toolkit is a complete browser-based utility for converting, generating and analyzing colors. It is designed for developers, designers and digital creators who need accurate color values, accessible contrast checks and practical palette generation.

  • Convert colors between HEX, RGB and HSL formats.
  • Generate random, similar and complementary colors.
  • Check WCAG contrast ratios and accessibility levels.
  • Create multiple types of color harmonies and palettes.
  • Save palettes locally in your browser.
  • Download generated palettes for later use.
  • Process all color data locally without uploading anything.
