Cron Expression Syntax

A standard cron expression contains five fields separated by spaces:

minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week
  • * matches every allowed value.
  • , separates multiple values, such as 1,15,30.
  • - defines a range, such as 1-5.
  • / defines an interval, such as */10.

This generator targets the common five-field Linux and Unix cron format. Some platforms use additional fields or platform-specific syntax.

Cron Expression Generator FAQ

What is a cron expression?

A cron expression defines when a command, script or automated task should run.

Which cron format does this tool generate?

It generates the common five-field format containing minute, hour, day of month, month and day of week.

Can I use these expressions with Linux cron?

Yes. The output is designed for common Linux and Unix cron implementations. Always verify whether your specific service supports the same syntax.

Why can day of month and day of week behave differently?

Traditional cron implementations may treat these two fields with OR-style matching when both are restricted. The final behavior depends on the cron implementation being used.

Is my schedule uploaded?

No. Expression generation, validation and preview calculation happen directly in your browser.
