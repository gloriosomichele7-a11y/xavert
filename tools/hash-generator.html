About


        

          The XAVERT Hash Generator creates MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384 and
          SHA-512 hashes from text directly in your browser.
        


        
Main Features


        
    
          
  • Generate MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384 and SHA-512 hashes.
    • 
          
  • Generate one algorithm or all supported algorithms at once.
    • 
          
  • Export results as plain text or JSON.
    • 
          
  • Count Unicode characters and UTF-8 bytes.
    • 
          
  • Copy or download generated results.
    • 
          
  • Process all input locally in the browser.
    • 
        


        
Algorithm Guidance


        

          SHA-256 and SHA-512 are appropriate choices for modern integrity
          workflows. MD5 and SHA-1 are included for compatibility and should not
          be used for new security-sensitive designs.
        


        
Privacy First


        

          Everything is processed locally within your browser. Your text and
          generated hashes are never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on
          any server.
