About

The XAVERT Case Converter transforms text into common writing and programming case styles directly in your browser.

Main Features

  • Convert text to lowercase, UPPERCASE and sentence case.
  • Generate Title Case and Capitalized Case.
  • Generate camelCase and PascalCase identifiers.
  • Generate snake_case and SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE.
  • Generate kebab-case and SCREAMING-KEBAB-CASE.
  • Generate dot.case and path/case.
  • Create alternating and inverse case text.
  • Copy or download the converted result.

Privacy First

Case conversion happens locally within your browser. Your text is never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on any server.
