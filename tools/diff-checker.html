About

The XAVERT Diff Checker compares two text versions directly in your browser and highlights line-level additions, removals and changes.

Main Features

  • Compare text using line-aware alignment.
  • Detect changed, added and removed lines.
  • Calculate similarity and difference statistics.
  • Optionally ignore case, whitespace or blank lines.
  • Copy or download the comparison report.
  • Process all text locally in the browser.

Privacy First

Everything is processed locally within your browser. Your text and comparison results are never uploaded, transmitted, shared or stored on any server.
