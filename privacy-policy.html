Privacy Policy

How XAVERT handles information when you use our online tools.

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Overview

XAVERT provides browser-based utilities for developers, documents, images, data conversion and everyday digital tasks. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit xavert.com and use XAVERT tools.

2. Browser-Based Processing

Many XAVERT tools are designed to process text, files or other input directly in your browser. When a tool specifically states that processing is local or browser-based, the relevant content is intended to remain on your device rather than being uploaded to XAVERT for processing.

Because individual tools may have different technical requirements, any privacy statement displayed on a specific tool should be read together with this Privacy Policy.

3. Accounts and Information You Provide

XAVERT currently does not require users to create an account to use its public tools. If you contact XAVERT by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address, message content and any information or attachments you voluntarily include.

4. Technical and Server Information

Like most websites, XAVERT's hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information needed to deliver, secure and maintain the website. This may include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and security or diagnostic logs.

Such information may be used for website operation, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, security and performance monitoring.

5. Cookies, Analytics and Similar Technologies

XAVERT may use technologies that are technically necessary for the operation, security or functionality of the website. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, this Privacy Policy and any required consent mechanisms will be updated accordingly.

XAVERT does not currently make a blanket claim that every third-party service or hosting component operates without cookies or similar technologies.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

XAVERT may rely on third-party providers for services such as hosting, infrastructure, security or email delivery, and may contain links to external websites. Third parties may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. XAVERT is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites that you choose to visit.

7. How Information May Be Used

Information processed in connection with XAVERT may be used to:

  • provide and maintain the website and its tools;
  • respond to support requests and communications;
  • protect the website against abuse, fraud and security threats;
  • diagnose technical problems and improve reliability;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Data Sharing and Sale

XAVERT does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide communications, maintain security or comply with legal requirements.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, including operational, security, support and legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service involved.

10. Security

XAVERT uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect the website and information processed through it. However, no internet service or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights regarding your personal information, which can include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, or request information about how your data is handled.

To make a privacy-related request, contact support@xavert.com. We may need to verify a request where reasonably necessary before acting on it.

12. Children's Privacy

XAVERT is a general-purpose utility website and is not specifically directed to children. Users should not submit personal information through support communications unless appropriate to do so.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as XAVERT develops, adds new features or services, or changes its technical or legal requirements. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

14. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy-related requests, contact XAVERT at support@xavert.com.
